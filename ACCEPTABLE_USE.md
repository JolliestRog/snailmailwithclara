# Community agreement

This service is for invited adults sending consensual personal mail.

Do not use it for harassment, threats, hate, sexual material sent without explicit consent, fraud, commercial solicitation, doxxing, surveillance, impersonation, dangerous substances, illegal goods, or evading a block or suspension. Do not share, sell, republish, or retain an address beyond the purpose for which it was granted.

Recipients may deny requests without explanation, pause participation, block members, and report unwanted mail. Anonymous roulette is not anonymous to the moderation process after a report. Moderators may suspend accounts while investigating safety concerns.

The project is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Riot Fest.
